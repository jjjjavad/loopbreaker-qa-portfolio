# Release summary

Published v41 is free to play on Roblox. Normal campaign progress still determines which chambers can be continued or replayed.

Studio evidence supports three specific fixes: safe room handoffs at the tested network profiles, restored replay access with checkpoint preservation, and initial gamepad controls. The twelve-chamber automated route run applies to its November 4 development build. The v41 recheck covers the title, intro, and Chamber 01 repair and replay. | Open area | Acceptance check |
|---|---|
| Live network | Cross exits in the published Roblox client. Log falls, readiness and input tail latency under real network variation. |
| Persistence | Complete, disconnect, and reconnect on an authorized test account. Check Continue, Replay, and recovery from an interrupted save or secured ending. |
| Devices | Complete representative controls and a chamber on physical controller and handset hardware. Record device and join configuration. |
